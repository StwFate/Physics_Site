## How to Set Up Your Google Slides

You have two easy ways to get this presentation into Google Slides:

### Method 1: Automated via Google Apps Script (Fastest — 30 Seconds)
1. Open Google Slides in your browser by going to [slides.new](https://slides.new).
2. In the top menu bar, click **Extensions** → **Apps Script**.
3. Select and delete any placeholder code in the script editor.
4. Open the file [`create_google_slides.gs`](file:///c:/Users/Venus/OneDrive/Desktop/Documents/School/Car_Buying_Project/create_google_slides.gs), copy everything, paste it into the editor, and click **Save** (disk icon).
5. Click **Run**. (If Google prompts for permissions, click *Review Permissions* → select your account → *Advanced* → *Go to Untitled project* → *Allow*).
6. Go back to your Google Slides tab: all 6 slides are generated with proper layouts and speaker notes!
7. Replace the gray image placeholder boxes with your photos from `Car_Buying_Project/images/`.

### Method 2: Manual Copy & Paste
If you prefer picking your own Google Slides theme template, copy and paste the clean slide contents below.
